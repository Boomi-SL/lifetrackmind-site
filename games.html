Calm games

Something slow for busy hands

Six small games. No timer, no score to beat, nothing to win. Play for as long as it helps.

  • Bubble pop

    Pop slow, drifting bubbles. More always come.

  • Memory match

    Find six pairs of quiet symbols at your own pace.

  • Mirror doodle

    Draw one line and watch it bloom into a pattern.

  • Shade sort

    Put seven shades in order, lightest to darkest.

  • Echo

    Watch the tiles light up, then repeat the pattern.

  • Ripple pond

    Tap the water. Watch the ripples spread and fade.
