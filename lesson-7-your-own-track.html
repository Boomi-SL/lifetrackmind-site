Your own track

A simple plan for the next month.

Reading about change feels like progress. It is not, yet. What changes things is a small action repeated until it becomes ordinary.

Big plans usually fail in the second week. A small plan you actually keep will take you further than a perfect one you drop.

So for the next month, pick only three things: one habit to practice, one boundary to hold, and one regular check-in with yourself. That is enough.

You will miss days. Everyone does. Missing a day is not the end of the plan. Giving up after missing a day is.

Try this today

  1. One habit: choose the exercise from these lessons that helped most and do it daily. Two minutes is fine.
  2. One boundary: choose one thing you will say no to, or do less of, this month.
  3. One check-in: once a week, ask “What felt lighter this week? What felt heavier?”
  4. Write the three down and put them where you will see them.

Small steps still move you forward.
